* StarIntel

StarIntel is a local-first intelligence platform for collecting information,
keeping its sources attached, and turning it into conclusions that people can
inspect. It combines a typed document model, supervised actors, research
tools, and explicit verification and approval steps.

The platform is built as cooperating projects. Each project owns a specific
part of the work, while shared document contracts and recorded provenance let
their outputs connect without hiding where a claim came from.

* How the platform works

1. *Collect*: actors gather records and retain source references, collection
   context, and timestamps.
2. *Structure*: documents use declared types and pinned contracts so that
   consumers can validate what they read.
3. *Investigate*: Auto Dig follows a bounded investigation plan; Quasar gives
   operators a graph workspace for reviewing documents and relationships.
4. *Review*: Prolog rules check workflow state and declared invariants.
   Research remains a proposal until the required human approval is recorded.
5. *Publish*: accepted material moves through an explicit, provenance-bearing
   publication path.

| Project | Responsibility |

* Document contracts

StarIntel has more than one pinned document consumer, so a version number only
has meaning alongside the contract and consumer that selected it. The platform
document page explains the website's v0.9 contract: release 0.9.1 over the
immutable 0.9.0 base schema. A separate page describes the StarLang 0.10.1
records read by the social-presence findings adapter.

* Operating principles

- *Provenance stays with the record.* Sources, evidence, and assessment are
  available to downstream reviewers.
- *Unknown is not verified.* Validation and approval state must be explicit.
- *Automation has boundaries.* Workers can collect and propose; publication
  follows its declared review gate.
- *Contracts are pinned.* Consumers select a release from their repository
  lock and preserve compatibility deliberately.
- *Results remain inspectable.* Plans, outputs, and checks are kept as data
  that operators can revisit.
