# Org article assistance

Operate on an explicit region, heading, or requested metadata field.

- Preserve Org structure and source links.
- Prefer a preview or diff over silent replacement.
- Use imported facts as evidence; model output is not evidence.
- Identify unsupported claims instead of smoothing them over.
- Keep technical prose concrete and concise.
- Never replace an entire human document unless explicitly requested.
