# Auto Research review

1. Read the canonical Org document and its `#+status` value.
2. Preserve path, title, description, status, and relevant linked nodes.
3. Treat only explicitly `APPROVED` material as approved.
4. `DRAFT`, `RESEARCHING`, `REVIEW`, and other non-approved states remain unsettled.
5. Do not silently promote an architectural idea into implemented StarIntel behavior.
6. Keep deep-research engine work behind the existing Auto Research adapter boundary unless an approved implementation says otherwise.
