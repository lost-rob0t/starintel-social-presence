* The workspace idea

The Quasar research treats the graph as one projection of an investigation,
not the investigation itself. The same identifiers can surface as:

- a graph, for structure questions
- a table, for scan and sort questions
- a map, for geo questions
- a timeline, for sequencing questions
- a document viewer, for the exact passage behind a claim

* Open questions

- Where does selection state live when a projection changes?
- How do saved queries round-trip between projections?
- What does undo mean across an actor boundary?

These questions are unverified research notes; do not cite this page as a
statement of shipped behavior.
